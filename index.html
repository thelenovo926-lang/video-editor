Auto SFX for video

Upload a clip. The page finds scene cuts, then adds a dark drone, whooshes on cuts, and a riser, silence and impact hit at the end. Everything runs in your browser; your video is never uploaded.

What to add

SFX volume
Cut sensitivity (higher finds more cuts)
Claude looks at frames from your video (a book, a page turning, a door, footsteps...) and adds matching sounds.

Export plays the video in real time while recording, so a 30 second clip takes about 30 seconds. Keep this tab open. It works in desktop Chrome, Edge and Firefox. The result is a .webm file; use the WAV in CapCut or another editor if you need MP4.
